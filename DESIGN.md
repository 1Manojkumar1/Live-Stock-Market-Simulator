# Professional Minimalist Fintech Design System

## Brand & Style
This design system is anchored in "Calculated Precision." It prioritizes density, clarity, and structural discipline. It is designed for professional traders who need information at a glance without the "oversized" distraction.

## Colors
- **Primary**: #09090B (Zinc-950) - Used for primary text and main buttons.
- **Surface**: #FFFFFF - Pure white for maximum clarity.
- **Surface-Muted**: #F9FAFB (Gray-50) - For background sectioning.
- **Success**: #10B981 - Sharp emerald for growth.
- **Danger**: #EF4444 - Crisp red for alerts.
- **Border**: #E5E7EB (Gray-200) - Fine lines for structure.

## Typography (Normal Scale)
- **Primary Font**: Inter (Humanist Sans)
- **Display**: 28px, Bold, -0.02em tracking.
- **Headline**: 18px, Semi-bold.
- **Body**: 14px, Regular.
- **Caption**: 12px, Medium, Uppercase (for labels).

## Shapes
- **Professional Rounding**: 
  - Cards/Containers: 8px (ROUND_TWO)
  - Buttons/Inputs: 6px

## Spacing (Compact)
- **Base Unit**: 4px
- **Card Padding**: 16px to 20px (was 32px)
- **Gaps**: 12px for small elements, 24px for major sections.

## Components
- **Buttons**: Smaller padding (8px 16px), 14px font size.
- **Cards**: Sharp 8px corners, 1px Gray-200 border, very subtle shadow.
- **Tables**: Reduced row height (44px), 13px font size for data.
